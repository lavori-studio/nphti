Why Clinical Hypnosis for DGBI?

Disorders of gut-brain interaction, including functional abdominal pain, irritable bowel syndrome, and functional dyspepsia, are among the most common reasons children and teens see a pediatric gastroenterologist. Symptoms are real and often disabling, leaving families searching for answers.

Because DGBI involve heightened sensitivity along the gut-brain axis, they respond well to approaches that calm that pathway. Gut-directed hypnotherapy is one of the best-studied psychological treatments for pediatric abdominal pain, with research showing meaningful, lasting relief. This meetup will deepen your skills in applying it.
