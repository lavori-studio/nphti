NPHTI Scholarship Campaign 2026–27

Supporting Pediatric Providers for the Next Generation

For many years, NPHTI has granted scholarships to help deserving professionals from around the world attend our training at a greatly reduced fee. The impact has been immeasurable, considering the number of scholarships granted and the number of young lives touched by those pediatric health professionals newly trained in clinical hypnosis. We are committed to continuing to provide impact like this!

Can you help us help deserving professionals attend NPHTI training? Your donations to our 501(c)(3) organization may provide you a tax deduction (please verify with your accountant), and will certainly bring a smile to many faces! Every contribution, big or small, makes a meaningful impact!
