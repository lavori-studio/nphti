National Pediatric Hypnosis Training Institute

About NPHTI.

NPHTI is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit educational organization that serves as a resource for health professionals and parents on therapeutic hypnosis — helping children and teens build resilience and overall well-being.

Our purpose

Mission & Vision

Our vision

Children will develop the skills to regulate their emotions, behavior, and mind-body reactions — and be confident in their capacities.

NPHTI’s mission is to be an educational and communication resource for health professionals and parents regarding therapeutic hypnosis, to maximize children’s and teens’ resilience and overall well-being.

Our approach

How We Fulfill Our Mission

Training Program

Ongoing clinical hypnosis skill development for advanced practice pediatric clinicians — helping children and teens build self-regulation, optimize resilience related to medical and mental health issues and stressful experiences, and maximize well-being and peak performance.

Resource Center

A place for parents and professionals to locate NPHTI-trained clinicians through our Find a Provider directory, and to access literature and media resources reflecting the most current clinical and research information in the field of pediatric hypnosis.

Community

A global network of hypnosis-trained child health clinicians who share a vision of empowering healthy and ill children through self-hypnosis, fostering self-regulation and resilience.

Since 1976

Our History

NPHTI’s Roots

The first workshop devoted solely to teaching hypnosis with children was held in Philadelphia in 1976, led by Gail Gardner, PhD and Karen Olness, MD — work that grew into the field’s foundational text, Hypnosis and Hypnotherapy with Children (Gardner & Olness, 1981). Annual introductory workshops began in 1987 at the Society for Developmental and Behavioral Pediatrics, taught for 25 years by a small group of clinician-educators: Karen Olness, Daniel Kohen, Candace Erickson, Leora Kuttner, Jud Reaney, and Lonnie Zeltzer, later joined by Rev. James Warnke, Melanie Gold, Laurence Sugarman, Pamela Kaiser, and Bob Deutsch. Intermediate and Advanced workshops followed in 1988 and 1989 to meet growing demand.

Founding NPHTI

In 2009, this core faculty formalized their work as the National Pediatric Hypnosis Training Institute. Co-founders Pamela Kaiser, PhD, CPNP, CNS and Daniel Kohen, MD, FAAP, ABMH set out to build the premier pediatric-specific hypnosis training organization in North America — designing a curriculum grounded in current research on adult learning and visual presentation, with the majority of training time devoted to hands-on skills practice rather than lecture.

Looking ahead

Continued Growth

  • Monthly webinar series — launched in August 2020 in response to the COVID-19 pandemic, taught by NPHTI faculty for clinicians with a pediatric hypnosis background, and still running today.
  • Regional workshops — hosted at U.S. children’s hospitals, bringing training closer to clinicians nationwide.
  • International workshops — multiple workshops abroad, extending NPHTI’s reach beyond the United States.
  • Expanded resources — a growing listserv, website, and literature and media library for both professionals and parents.
Leadership

Our Training Leadership

Co-founders Pamela Kaiser, PhD, CPNP, CNS and Daniel Kohen, MD, FAAP, ABMH shaped NPHTI’s curriculum with input from a founding group of Senior Faculty — Karen Olness, MD, FAAP, ABMH; Candace Erickson, MD, MPH, FAAP; Leora Kuttner, PhD; Laurence Sugarman, MD, ABMH, FAAP; Jud Reaney, MD, FAAP, BCB; and Bob Deutsch, PhD — along with additional inaugural faculty who joined in the years that followed. In 2016, Bonnie Boucher, HMCC, CMP joined as administrative and conference coordinator for NPHTI’s training program, and was invited to join the Board of Directors in 2019.
