Scholarships for the NPHTI Annual Workshops

NPHTI provides scholarships to help defray annual workshop registration costs for clinicians and trainees who have limited monetary resources.

Status The deadline to apply for a 2026 scholarship (August 15, 2026) has passed. Check back in 2027 for details on scholarships to the 2027 Annual Workshops.

Criteria for Recipients

NPHTI intends to focus its limited scholarships for the annual workshop on:

  • Trainees (graduate students, interns, residents, and post-doctoral fellows) in clinical programs leading to licensure
  • Licensed clinicians who live in resource-limited countries
  • U.S. licensed clinicians with limited financial resources
  • Early career professionals (e.g. within 2 years after licensure) with limited financial resources
  • Trainees or licensed clinicians with limited financial resources who demonstrate plans to conduct research in the area of pediatric clinical hypnosis within the next 2 years

The scholarship award does not go directly to the awardee — it goes to NPHTI for registration fees, or to pay hotel costs if hotel support is part of the award.
