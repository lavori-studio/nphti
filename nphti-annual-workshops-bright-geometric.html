Eligibility

Requires an active clinical license and one of the following:

  • Doctoral degree in Medicine, Psychology, Nursing, or Dentistry
  • Masters degree in Social Work, Marriage & Family Therapy, or Clinical/Counseling Psychology
  • Masters degree in Nursing or Advanced Practice Registered Nurse (APRN, CPNP, CNS)
  • Other advanced-degree clinicians (e.g. Occupational & Physical Therapists, Speech & Language Pathologists)
  • Child Life Specialists holding a doctoral, masters, or bachelors degree with CCLS certification
  • Music Therapists with MT-BC board certification
  • Registered or Board Certified Art Therapists (ATR / ATR-BC)
  • Full-time interns, residents, fellows, or graduate students, with a letter from their department confirming current status

Relevant clinical pediatric training and experience will also be considered on an individual basis — contact TrainWithNPHTI@gmail.com for more information.

Accreditation

Approved by the American Society of Clinical Hypnosis Standards of Training Committee toward Membership and Certification requirements. The ASCH Education & Research Foundation (ASCH-ERF) is accredited by the Accreditation Council for Continuing Medical Education and designates this activity for up to 21 AMA PRA Category 1 Credits™ (Fundamentals & Utilization) and 20 credits (Advanced Case-based Masterclass). Also approved by the National Association of Social Workers for 20 continuing education contact hours, and an approved PACE Program Provider for FAGD/MAGD credit.
