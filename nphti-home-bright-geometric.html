What We Do

Training Program

Ongoing clinical hypnosis skill development for advanced practice pediatric clinicians — helping children and teens build self-regulation, resilience, and overall well-being.

Resource Center

A place for parents and professionals to find NPHTI-trained clinicians and access current literature and research in pediatric hypnosis.

Community

A global network of hypnosis-trained child health clinicians, united around empowering children through self-regulation and resilience.

Our mission

Children benefit from calm, evidence-based tools for pain, anxiety, and medical fear when the professionals who treat them are trained to teach those tools.

NPHTI trains licensed health professionals in pediatric clinical hypnosis so that it becomes part of standard pediatric care.
